Product case · Retention & Lifecycle

RFM analysis SmartFlow

SmartFlow is a platform of AI salespeople for experts and small businesses that sell online products through messengers. Repeat purchases are falling even though new users keep arriving at a steady rate. I segmented buyers with RFM and built a plan to bring repeat purchases back, cut marketing spend by 20% and grow revenue by 10%.

The strategy

Growth from people who have already bought

New users keep arriving at a steady rate, so the problem starts after the first purchase. That is why I fixed repeat purchases first and then rebuilt the budget on top of that.

The strategy in one word
Retention
  • Problem30-day repeat purchase among new buyers fell from 23.1% to 15.7%, while the inflow of new buyers stayed the same — about 3,300 a month.
  • InsightIt dropped across every channel and niche right after July 20, when the post-payment message was removed from the funnel. 80% of Potential Loyalists slid into Hibernating.
  • SolutionThe Smart consultant and AI buddy guide each buyer to a second purchase; lapsed regulars get an offer matched to their new goal.

Budget & revenue

−20% spend and +11.7% revenue at the same time

RFM shows where money does not change behavior. Champions would buy without a discount, Hibernating barely respond, and paid ads aimed at our own base duplicate free channels. The retention budget used to be split by headcount, so 68% went to Hibernating, who buy at 0.3% a month. I move that money to where it creates lift.

Channels & approaches

Owned channels instead of paid ones

The biggest saving is to stop paying ad networks to reach people I can already reach through Telegram and notifications. Paid channels stay only where they are targeted.

Look-alike: find people similar to your best buyers

A look-alike audience is a group of people similar to a seed list. The ad network takes my best customers and finds new people with similar traits. It is available in Meta (Instagram, Facebook), TikTok and VK Ads.

How it works

The ad network does the modelling — my job is to give it a good seed.

  1. SeedI export Champions' contacts — phone numbers or emails. Adding purchase value lets the network favour people similar to those who spend more.
  2. UploadI upload the list to the ad account. Contacts are hashed, so the network matches them to its users without seeing raw data.
  3. ModellingThe algorithm learns what these people share: interests, behavior, demographics, devices, what they respond to.
  4. TargetingAds are shown to people with similar traits. I choose the size: 1% is the closest match, 10% is broader but less precise.
  5. ExclusionsI exclude current buyers and Hibernating so I don't pay to reach people I can already reach.
Why the seed is Champions, not all buyers. Similar to everyone means similar to one-time buyers too. Similar to Champions means similar to people who buy again. Same budget, more repeat buyers among the new ones. The data shows this by channel: 29.8% of new buyers from referrals repeat, versus 16.5% from TikTok.

12-week plan

From diagnosis to scale

Weeks 1–2Diagnosis

Cohorts, payment errors, changes over the last 2 months. Customer development: 5–7 interviews with Potential Loyalists and At Risk.

Weeks 3–6A/B tests

Second-purchase sequence, offers for At Risk, look-alike from Champions. Duration, sample size and success criteria set upfront.

Weeks 7–12Scale

Winning scenarios roll out to the whole segment. Ineffective channels get switched off. 10% of the audience stays as a control to check the effect holds.

About this case

What this case shows about how I work

Approach

  • RFM segmentation in SQL on the payments table in Supabase
  • Turning segments into decisions: budget, channels, product mechanics
  • Testable hypotheses and A/B tests with a control group
  • Business constraints: what the platform does vs what depends on experts

Limitations

  • The data is demo data generated by a model: SmartFlow does not yet have 12 months of payment history. All calculations are in the dashboard and the Excel file
  • R, F and M thresholds are tuned for each niche on real data
  • The lift is a target, not a guarantee; it is validated with experiments
