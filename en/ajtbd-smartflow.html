SmartFlow: money is lost between the steps

I ran 5 AJTBD interviews with experts, small businesses and marketers, built a job graph for three job types and turned the pains into 7 product changes prioritised with ICE. The key finding: customers hire SmartFlow not for a “bot” but for steady income without living in chats, and money is lost not on the first reply but on statuses and follow-ups.

Interviews53 segments: experts, small business, marketers
Job graph21 steps3 job types, 9 steps with pain
Current solutions score4.6 / 10average across 5 respondents
Priority #1ICE 512customer statuses in Office

What I found

  1. 1They hire “time + money”. All five share one higher job: steady income and free time. Positioning: “sells while you live”, not “an AI chatbot”.
  2. 2Losses happen between steps. Replying is half the job: the main losses are at “see the customer’s status” and “follow up non-buyers”.
  3. 3New segment: offline services. Salons and studios on WhatsApp need booking, not nurturing: a short script with value visible on day one.

What I propose

  1. 1Statuses in Office (ICE 512). 5 customer statuses and a “who to follow up today” push.
  2. 2Launch from a template in 10 minutes (504). A Smart consultant from a niche template replies 24/7.
  3. 3AI-generated funnel stages (294). The marketer writes a brief, AI drafts the stages, the marketer edits.
Method

5 interviews with one AJTBD template

Questions are about the last real case, not opinions. Screening: in the last 3 months the person handled messenger sales themselves.

Interview questions

  1. Context: what do you do, how do customers reach you now?
  2. Last case: recall your latest lead or sale in a messenger. What happened?
  3. Trigger: what made you look for a solution?
  4. Expected outcome: what should have changed?
  5. Steps: what did you do, from lead to payment?
  6. Struggles and emotions: where did you get stuck, what annoyed you?
  7. Current solution: what do you use now, why haven’t you switched?
  8. Higher job: why do you want more sales? (ask “why” 2–3 times)
  9. Criteria: what would be ideal? What would you pay for?
  10. Score: how satisfied are you with the current result, 1 to 10?
Interviews

What respondents told me

Each interview ends with a job statement “When…, I want…, so that…”. Tap a card to open all fields.

R1Online expert, marketing school (author)4/10
Context
“I ran an online marketing school for 3 years. Customers came from Instagram and Telegram, and the whole sale then happened in DMs.”
Last case
“I ran ads and got about 80 leads over a weekend. I replied myself until late at night. Half only got an answer the next day, and they had gone cold.”
Trigger
“I realised it wasn’t me selling, it was my tiredness. The more traffic, the worse the conversion.”
Expected outcome
Every lead gets an instant reply, and she steps in only for those ready to pay.
Steps
Lead → DM reply → questions about the programme → price → objections → payment link → manual reminders.
Struggles and emotions
Burnout, guilt towards her children, anger at herself for lost leads.
Current solution
A builder bot: “people typed questions and the bot broke. A manager is expensive and has to learn my approach.”
Higher job
“I don’t need more chats, I need steady income and evenings with my kids.”
Criteria
“A seller who knows my product like I do, replies instantly and calls me only when the person is ready to pay.”
Score
4/10

Job statement: When 80 leads arrive over a weekend and I’m alone, I want each to get an instant reply and to be called only for those ready to pay, so that I have steady income and evenings with my kids.

R2Nutrition expert with an assistant (persona)5/10
Context
“I run nutrition challenges. My assistant answers on WhatsApp.”
Last case
“My assistant went on holiday and sales nearly halved in 2 weeks. Everything was in her head.”
Trigger
Sales dropped because one person went on holiday.
Expected outcome
See every customer and their stage without asking the assistant.
Steps
Lead → assistant replies → call with the expert → payment → Excel sheet.
Struggles and emotions
Anxiety and loss of control: “I don’t know who bought, who is thinking, and who vanished.”
Current solution
Assistant + Excel: “the sheet is always out of date.”
Higher job
“I want to scale and not depend on one person.”
Criteria
“I don’t need a CRM with 100 buttons, I need to see who to follow up today.”
Score
5/10

Job statement: When my assistant is unavailable, I want to see every customer’s status and who to follow up today, so that I can grow without depending on one person.

R3Beauty salon, Taraz (persona)3/10
Context
“Bookings come through WhatsApp and Instagram; I or the administrator reply.”
Last case
“A client asked the price of colouring in the evening and got a reply in the morning. She had already booked another salon.”
Trigger
A booking lost because of an evening message.
Expected outcome
The client gets the price at once and books a free slot without the administrator.
Steps
Price question → reply → pick a time → write it in a notebook → reminder the day before.
Struggles and emotions
Irritation at repeated questions, tiredness: “they write at 11 pm.”
Current solution
WhatsApp Business auto-reply: “it just says ‘we’ll reply later’, that doesn’t help.”
Higher job
“So my stylists are fully booked and I’m not glued to my phone.”
Criteria
“If a bot tells the price and books the time itself, I’ll pay.”
Score
3/10

Job statement: When a client asks the price in the evening, I want them answered and booked at once, so that my stylists are fully booked.

R4Marketer and producer (persona)5/10
Context
“I run launches for experts: funnels, nurturing, chatbots.”
Last case
“I built a funnel for a psychologist in 3 weeks. For the next client, a coach, I had to redo almost everything from scratch.”
Trigger
An expert didn’t pay the rest because “the funnel didn’t work”, although there were plenty of leads.
Expected outcome
Build a funnel in days, not weeks, and prove the result with numbers.
Steps
Find a client → brief → script → copy and video → setup via a tech specialist → launch → report.
Struggles and emotions
Resentment and helplessness: “the expert sells badly on calls, and the funnel gets the blame.”
Current solution
A fixed fee per project, her own drafts in Google Docs, a bot via a tech specialist.
Higher job
“I want to earn from my assets, not sell hours.”
Criteria
“If my funnels sold as templates and I got a share of sales, I’d make them non-stop.”
Score
5/10

Job statement: When I take a client from a new niche, I want to build the funnel from a ready base and earn a share of sales, so that I earn from my assets, not my hours.

R5GetCourse / chatbot tech specialist (persona)6/10
Context
“I set up GetCourse, Salebot, payments.”
Last case
“A client asked for a bot that answers any question. On Salebot that’s hundreds of branches.”
Trigger
Clients increasingly ask for a “smart” bot like ChatGPT.
Expected outcome
The bot understands free text and edits take minutes.
Steps
Brief from the marketer → branch map → build in the builder → connect payments → tests → endless edits.
Struggles and emotions
Routine and burnout: “every edit costs money and time.”
Current solution
Salebot and GetCourse, because “clients are already there.”
Higher job
“I want to take more clients without burning out on routine setup.”
Criteria
“I need a tool where I set the logic and goals, and AI writes the texts.”
Score
6/10

Job statement: When a client asks for a “smart” bot, I want to set logic and goals instead of writing hundreds of branches, so that I can take more clients without burning out.
