Step by step

How I calculated it, from scratch

Each step is a sheet in Excel and a section here. Tap to open.

  1. Data → “Orders”All payments from Oct 1, 2025 to Sep 30, 2026. One row is one payment: customer, date, amount. In real work this is a SQL export from the payments table.
  2. By monthOrders, revenue, new and repeat. New buyers are stable → the problem is after the first purchase.
  3. CohortsCustomers by month of first purchase: what share came back after 1, 2, 3… months. The drop is in cohorts from July on.
  4. DiagnosticsRepeat before and after, by channel and niche. It fell everywhere → the cause is in the product: the post-payment message was removed on July 20.
  5. R, F, M → “Customers”R = Sep 30, 2026 − last purchase, F = number of orders, M = sum of orders.
  6. ThresholdsR is the 90th percentile of the interval to the second purchase, rounded up to 10 days. F is where the next-purchase rate plateaus. M is the top 25%.
  7. Segments and migration4 segments and movement between them from Jul 31 to Sep 30 — shows who we are losing.
  8. StrategyHow many people buy per month now and at target → extra buyers × revenue per buyer. Budget goes where there is lift.
