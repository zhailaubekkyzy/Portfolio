Audience

About two thirds of the drop is the new channels. Their users rarely reach day 30. Fix: hypothesis 2.

Product

The rest is Android on v2.0. First step: crash rate and book open time on v2.0, roll back or hotfix. Cheaper than any experiment.

Chronic

The second-book rate barely moved month to month. It doesn’t explain April, but it is the biggest growth lever. Fix: hypothesis 1.

About the data

A model that reproduces the case brief

The brief gives totals only. To practise on segments, I simulated every user so that the totals match the brief exactly.

Exact totals

New users 12/15/18/22/25/27K, active on day 30: 5,400 / 6,750 / 8,100 / 8,360 / 7,500 / 7,560. 65% finished, 30% of them started a second. Rating about 4.5 in Jan–Mar and 4.2 in Apr–Jun.

Illustrative assumptions

TikTok and a “free books” promo start in April. v2.0 ships on May 6 and performs worse on Android. Channels, platforms, genres, CAC and spend are invented to show how to find a cause.

How totals are pinned

Each user has a probability to stay, driven by channel, motivation, platform and version. A logit shift scales it so the expected sum equals the brief. Systematic sampling then picks exactly that many.
